A dedicated study guide and estate-planning reference repository for candidates preparing for the **AAFM Chartered Trust & Estate Planner (CTEP) Certification**, covering succession planning, wills, trusts, taxation, and wealth transfer.

---

## Exam Overview

The Chartered Trust & Estate Planner (CTEP) designation focuses on estate planning, succession laws, trust structures, and the transfer and protection of wealth. The AAFM India program develops knowledge for professionals advising clients on estate and succession planning within relevant legal frameworks.

- **Exam Format**: Online multiple-choice examination
- **Target Audience**: Financial advisors, wealth managers, private bankers, accountants, lawyers, and other estate-planning professionals
- **Prerequisites**: Candidates may register after completing 10+2 or equivalent; a graduation degree in any stream is required for the designation to be awarded

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Introduction to Estate Planning

- Estate-planning processes, objectives, and the role of the estate planner.
- Estate assessment, asset ownership, liquidity, and closure-cost considerations.
- Wealth transfer techniques and coordination among financial and legal professionals.

### 2. Laws Affecting Estate Planning

- Relevant provisions of property, partnership, and company law.
- Legal considerations affecting ownership, nominations, and succession.
- FEMA and other applicable rules affecting estate and wealth planning.

### 3. Personal & Intestate Succession Laws

- Hindu succession, HUF structures, and coparcenary rights.
- Muslim personal law and other applicable succession frameworks.
- Intestate succession, inheritance rights, and distribution of estate assets.

### 4. Wills & Testamentary Succession

- Wills, testamentary planning, and common limitations in estate documents.
- Probate, succession certificates, and executor responsibilities.
- Asset titling, nominations, and coordination between estate-planning instruments.

### 5. Trust Planning in India

- Private, family, testamentary, and discretionary trusts.
- Trust formation, trustee and protector responsibilities, and trust administration.
- Trust taxation and planning for asset protection and beneficiary needs.

### 6. International Estate Planning

- NRI and OCI succession considerations and cross-border estate structures.
- FEMA-related considerations for inheritance and repatriation.
- International trust and estate planning across relevant jurisdictions.
